EZPZ builds synthetic markets and utility games on top of existing liquidity on Polymarket, Kalshi, and [pump.fun](http://pump.fun), rather than competing with those platforms directly. This extender market explanation covers the [pump.fun](http://pump.fun)-native mechanism: the POT (proof-of-transaction) system, which layers a synthetic binary prediction market on top of any live token.

The mechanism is an extension of a synthetic prediction market in which creators choose from pre-set parameters tied to near-future events that could plausibly happen to their token (for example, a price move within a time window, bonding-curve timing, red candles, or liquidity pumps).
