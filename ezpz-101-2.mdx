EZPZ builds synthetic markets and utility games on top of existing liquidity on Polymarket, Kalshi, and [pump.fun](http://pump.fun), rather than competing with those platforms directly. This extender market explanation covers the [pump.fun](http://pump.fun)-native mechanism: the POT (proof-of-transaction) system, which layers a synthetic binary prediction market on top of any live token.

The mechanism is an extension of a synthetic prediction market in which creators choose from pre-set parameters tied to near-future events that could plausibly happen to their token (for example, a price move within a time window, bonding-curve timing, red candles, or liquidity pumps). Presently, we have pre-set parameters for creators to choose from. We will add more options in the near future.

# Live integration

EZPZ maintains a direct live feed of every token being created and traded on [pump.fun](http://pump.fun). When a token eg call it "Meme Abc" launches, it is reflected on EZPZ in real time, and its creator (or any user) can open synthetic markets on it. An initial pre-made set of parameters keeps this simple before creators graduate to full customization.

With zero-decay in price differentials, creators and users alike will be to capitalize on our offerings and maximize their returns accordingly.

# PoT Mechanism

Once a token is live, the mechanism runs in four stages:

![EZPZ POT Mechanism Flow](/images/EZPZ_POT_Mechanism_Flow.png "EZPZ POT Mechanism Flow")

This flowchart depicts any coin that is on Solana, predominantly [pump.fun](http://pump.fun) tokens.

-Creator sets synthetic prediction market and picks a pre-set parameter tied to a near-term event on their token.<br />-Creator provides USDC or SOL for synthetic markets. Locks any amount of their choosing into a POT via smart contract, as a signal of conviction to fund payouts.<br />-Users can choose to predict whether it will be a yes or no and participants use either USDC or SOL alike for the market.<br />-Market resolves and closes. Wins and losses are added to or deducted from the POT accordingly.

EZPZ has a fully integrated swapping and bridging mechanism so as to allow users a seamless trading experience. Users will be able to trade with and pay with USDC alone. Everything feature is created so users do not need to toggle across different platforms.
