# Creator flow

![Ezpz Two Action Funding Flow](/images/ezpz_two_action_funding_flow.png "Ezpz Two Action Funding Flow")

The creator flow shows no shared node, no auto-conversion. Just two independent deposits that the creator makes on their own via a smart contract interaction, EZPZ does NOT hold anyone's funds or deposits ever. Whenever the creator is ready to open each side of the product, simple contract logic and it lets you ship the market and the game features on different timelines if needed. 

# User accessibility

We understand how frustrating it is to switch between different apps and websites to track, trade, swap and convert assets to desired ones. To allow smoother and faster accessibility, EZPZ has connected numerous swaps which even includes purchasing of [pump.fun](http://pump.fun) tokens directly on our page if needed. Moreover we will be integrating additional bridges so users across other chains can enjoy the unique benefits offered by EZPZ. 

Users can now trade automatically without the need for SOL for gas, EZPZ automatically converts a small portion of your USDC for your gas fees for each unique transaction. Promoting a seamless and fun experience on EZPZ.
